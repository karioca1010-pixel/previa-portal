Avx Logistica Ltda

AVX Logística was founded in 1998 and operates as an international cargo freight forwarder, specialized in the Oil & Gas market. Over these years, we have kept the same commercial and operational team, developing strategies that ensure excellence in personalized service to our clients across air, sea and road modes. We specialize in implementing tailor-made solutions.

We offer FLEXIBILITY and RELIABILITY, plus a wide range of services, aimed at reducing costs and creating efficiency models that ensure savings and speed in the final result.

Our network is made up of agents around the world, wherever your cargo originates. They are able to handle cargo checks, storage, door-to-door service and special project logistics, according to your needs.

AVX LOGÍSTICA IS A TWO-TIME CHAMPION (2022 and 2023) of the logistics efficiency award at Rio Galeão.

PEL is an annual award honoring companies that stood out in logistics sectors (airlines, cargo agents, importers, customs brokers and carriers). Together with one of our clients, awarded as Outstanding Importer, we were recognized for speed in clearance procedures with GTG.

“AVX logística, together with its partner customs broker, achieved an average cargo release time at Rio Galeão of 7 hours and 8 minutes, a 14% improvement over last year, when it received its first award.”

Recognized Commitment, Proven Results.

At AVX Logística, our commitment is to deliver efficiency and agility at every step of the logistics process. Two-time winner of the Rio Galeão Logistics Efficiency Award (2022 and 2023), we prove that turning challenges into solutions is part of our essence. With dedication, expertise and solid partnerships, we ensure that cargo release is faster and more reliable, exceeding results every year. Count on AVX Logística to raise the performance of your operations and ensure excellence in transport and logistics management.
