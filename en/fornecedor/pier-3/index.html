PIER 3

Founded in 2013, PIER III is a project structuring firm that offers specialized engineering and consulting services for the port infrastructure and logistics sectors. The company is headquartered in Curitiba/PR and has a team of highly qualified professionals, such as engineers and project managers with extensive experience in the field, as well as several specialist partners who are hired according to the specific needs of each client/project. Engineering

We offer complete engineering services for port and infrastructure projects, from preliminary studies to detailed executive engineering. Our services include preliminary studies and preliminary designs, conceptual and basic engineering, executive or detailed engineering, project quality control (CQP), Owner’s engineering and advisory for hiring design and construction firms.

Consulting

Our consulting area specializes in technical, economic and environmental feasibility studies (EVTEA), port planning and master plans. We provide technical advisory for concessions, PPPs, contract rebalancing and extensions, in addition to carrying out multidisciplinary technical due diligence. We develop market studies, demand forecasting, go-to-market research, operational studies and detailed simulations for optimizing multimodal transport networks.

Management

We work on full project management and construction supervision, setting up a Project Management Office (PMO) and managing CapEx, budgets and contractual claims. Our experience ensures effective control of processes and delivery within the established deadlines and specifications.

Competitive Advantages

With two decades of experience, PIER III stands out for its ability to develop customized solutions for each client. Our innovative approach combines specialized technical knowledge with our own work methodology, ensuring consistent and excellent project delivery. We work with more realistic and therefore more effective strategies, always guiding suitable and efficient solutions to understand how our clients' processes, standards and activity structures work.
