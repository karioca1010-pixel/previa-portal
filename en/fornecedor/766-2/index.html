TPAR

TPAR is a terminal specialized in Port Logistics and Onshore Logistics Base services. We are duly licensed to support the execution of Offshore Projects. The Port of Angra dos Reis is a Public Port, leased to the Splenda group through its subsidiary TPAR, Terminal Portuário de Angra dos Reis S/A. The main comparative advantages are its strategic location, close to the pre-salt, which allows fuel consumption to be cut by up to 40%, the 400 meter quay, and a draft able to receive large vessels such as PLSVs, drilling rigs, MODUs and Heavy Lift ships. The Terminal's Board of Directors is made up of executives Paulo Narcélio Amaral, Leandro Cariello and Stephanos Velissaropoulos.

Our Services: TPAR specializes in logistics support for Offshore activities and in handling project cargo and general cargo. It has a team of highly qualified professionals and offers complete infrastructure to serve its clients.

Port Operation The quay is 400 linear meters long, with bollards every 30 meters. All are certified with a capacity of 55 and 65 SWL.

Waste Treatment TPAR has qualified partners for the transport and final disposal of waste of any class, as well as for tank cleaning.

Offshore Support Base TPAR specializes in handling pipes, risers, project cargo and supporting offshore operations.

Fluids Plant Our fluids plant began operating in 2011 with a storage capacity of 43 thousand barrels and production of 6 thousand barrels per day.

Water, Power and Diesel Supply Capacity to meet our clients' required demand at any point in the Port.

Cargo Storage TPAR has an area of 88,000 m², of which 48,000 m² is bonded yard, 21,000 m² is non-bonded yard, and a 2,250 m² masonry warehouse.

Close to the Paraíba Valley, the main state capitals and the pre-salt fields of the Santos Basin.

Fully qualified staff trained in international standards and guidelines for Offshore service.

Large availability of local hotel rooms to support crews, contractors and administrative staff.

Strategic Solutions for Complex Challenges in Port Logistics and Offshore Support

At TPAR, we understand that every operation has its particularities. That is why our commitment is to go beyond the basic service, offering consultative support that helps our clients optimize their processes, reduce costs and ensure the flow of their operations. TPAR is your solution for a more efficient, worry-free logistics future.
