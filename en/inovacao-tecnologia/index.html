Why innovate in foreign trade?

The foreign trade sector is constantly changing and demands ever more agility, precision and operational intelligence. Innovation is essential to cut costs, automate processes, improve document management, integrate systems and create more competitiveness for companies in Rio de Janeiro and across Brazil. With this initiative, Portal ComexRio positions itself as a connection hub between local companies, sector operators and startups that deliver technology and innovation. Our goal is to bring those who need to innovate closer to those who develop real solutions to market challenges. This connection strengthens the local ecosystem, creates business opportunities and encourages the modernization of the sector.

Partners driving innovation

Discover companies and startups that are transforming foreign trade with technology, automation and data intelligence.

Compensei

Compensei is a Brazilian ClimateTech startup dedicated to decarbonizing businesses, public institutions and events. Our mission is to democratize climate management through technology and innovative solutions that help organizations measure, monitor, reduce and offset greenhouse gas emissions. We act strategically and educationally to generate positive environmental impact, increase competitiveness and strengthen our clients' sustainable reputation, contributing to a more resilient, low-carbon economy.

Learn More

Labsecreto

Lab Secreto is a consultancy specialized in developing solutions for the industrial, logistics and port sectors. We combine process and operations experience with technologies such as Artificial Intelligence, Digital Twins and custom systems to increase companies' efficiency and competitiveness. We work in partnership with our clients to simplify operations, reduce bottlenecks and accelerate digital transformation, creating scalable solutions that deliver concrete results, drive innovation and strengthen sustainable business growth.

Litro de Luz

Litro de Luz Brasil is a social organization dedicated to bringing sustainable lighting and access to clean energy to vulnerable communities. Our mission is to promote inclusion, safety and community development through low-cost, sustainable and replicable solar solutions. We work collaboratively with residents, sharing knowledge and training communities to lead the implementation and maintenance of the technologies. Combining social innovation and renewable energy, we work to improve quality of life, strengthen local autonomy and generate lasting positive impact in regions without adequate access to electricity.

Delta Entech

Delta Entech is a Brazilian deeptech specialized in developing regenerative technologies for environmental remediation. The company offers advanced solutions for the treatment of water, effluents, stormwater drainage, contaminated soils and atmospheric emissions, using smart mineral-based materials capable of capturing and removing contaminants such as oils, heavy metals, phosphorus, organic compounds and polluting gases. Focused on innovation, sustainability and the circular economy, Delta Entech serves industries, ports, logistics operators, sanitation companies and infrastructure companies seeking to reduce environmental impacts and strengthen their ESG strategies.

Métis

Métis is an artificial intelligence platform that helps field teams prepare contextualized risk analyses (APR and PT) aligned with the real conditions of the work being performed. The platform integrates procedures, standards, training records, lessons learned and operational information to generate more accurate risk assessments that fit the reality of the workplace.

Designed for frontline workers, Métis enables better activity planning, supports field decision-making and contributes to safer, more efficient and more consistent execution.

NSGreen Fuels

NSGreen Fuels is a technology-based startup and UFMG spin-off that develops solutions to accelerate the energy transition in the aviation, road transport and maritime transport sectors. Its proprietary technology converts waste oils and other complex feedstocks into Green Diesel (HVO) and Sustainable Aviation Fuel (SAF), with high efficiency and no need for pre-treatment. The company offers complete technology packages, integrating catalysts, processes and technical support to expand renewable fuel production with greater flexibility, lower cost and national innovation.

FlowPrint

FlowPrint Innovations is a technology-based startup incubated at COPPE/UFRJ, specialized in developing national solutions for laboratory automation, real-time monitoring and industrial Internet of Things (IoT). The company integrates its own hardware, sensors, software, web dashboards and additive manufacturing to develop modular, customizable and lower-cost equipment.

Its solutions include FlowSense, for monitoring gases, temperature, humidity and pressure; FlowTemp, for continuous temperature monitoring; FlowStock, for reagent management and traceability; as well as syringe pumps and continuous-flow chemistry systems. FlowPrint serves laboratories, R&D centers and industries, promoting safety, operational efficiency and sustainability.

SEALS Solutions

SEALS Solutions develops technology solutions for managing the flow of passengers and crew on large vessels, serving the needs of the port sector and maritime logistics. Made up of specialists in software, information security and port operations, the company automates processes and optimizes control of the movement of people.

Its main solution is SEALS Control, an integrated platform for managing passengers and crew, automating processes and meeting regulatory compliance. The company serves vessels such as tugboats, cargo ships, bulk carriers, offshore platforms, merchant ships and cruise ships, providing greater control, traceability and operational efficiency.
