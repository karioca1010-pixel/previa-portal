Port Terminals

Rio de Janeiro concentrates important port terminals and logistics operators that support offshore activities, offering infrastructure, storage, cargo handling and integrated solutions for the oil and gas industry.

Main Offshore Port Terminals and Logistics Operators

Gávea Group

GÁVEA GROUP operates in the logistics and port infrastructure sector, offering complete solutions for the Oil & Gas industry and international trade. With an integrated operating model, we serve clients throughout Brazil, with a strategic presence in Rio de Janeiro, Espírito Santo and the Northeast.

Angra dos Reis Port Terminal

The mission of Terminal Portuário de Angra dos Reis (TPAR) is to offer safe, efficient and sustainable logistics solutions for the oil and gas chain. With a strategic location close to the main pre-salt fields, TPAR reduces operating costs and stands out as the only port operator at the site, ensuring agility in offshore support and general cargo handling.

Its integrated operations cover 24x7 port operations, storage over an area of 88,839 m² and environmental management, actively contributing to the sustainable development of Rio de Janeiro. The commitment to excellence is reflected in the zero accidents milestone and in the promotion of innovation and social and environmental responsibility.
