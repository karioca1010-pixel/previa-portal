Multirio Container Terminal

High-capacity container terminal with modern infrastructure for handling large volumes, equipped to receive large vessels and offering high operating productivity with an annual capacity of 1 million TEUs.

Request more information

Location and Access

Terminal de Contêineres Multirio is located in the Port of Rio de Janeiro, with sea access via Guanabara Bay and integrated land connectivity. Its infrastructure allows operations with large vessels and high operational efficiency.

Sea access through Guanabara Bay

15.3-meter draft for large vessels

Road and rail connectivity

Proximity to urban and industrial centers
