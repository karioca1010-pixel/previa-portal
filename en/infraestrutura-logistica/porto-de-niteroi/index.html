Port of Niterói

Port terminal specialized in ship repair and offshore support cargo handling, strategically located in Guanabara Bay with excellent road and maritime connectivity to serve the oil sector.

Request more information

Location and Access

The Port of Niterói is located on Guanabara Bay, with privileged road access and a sheltered maritime entrance. Its strategic location eases offshore operations and ship repair activities, with direct connectivity to the main access routes.

Protected entrance to Guanabara Bay

Access channel 18.5 km long

Road access via federal and state highways

Proximity to offshore bases

Facilities and Equipment

Road Access

  • Federal Highway BR-040

Direct connection to the federal road network.

  • State Highways RJ-071 and RJ-083

Easy regional access through state highways.

  • Via Avenida Brasil

Urban access through Rio de Janeiro's main expressway.

Sea Access

  • Entrance to Guanabara Bay

Sheltered sea access with an average depth of 20 meters.

  • Access Channel

Channel 18.5 km long and 150 meters wide, bounded by the lighthouses of Morro do Pão de Açúcar and Fortaleza de Santa Cruz.

  • Operating Draft: 7.5 meters

Adequate depth for offshore support vessels and repair ships.

Port Facilities

  • Operational Area: 21,900 m²

Suitable space for specialized port operations.

  • Quay

Infrastructure for berthing vessels with drafts of up to 7.5 meters.

Specialized Services

  • Ship Repair Activities

Facilities for maintenance and repair of various vessels.

  • Offshore Cargo Handling

Specialization in cargo supporting oil industry activities:

  • Supplies for platforms
  • Specialized parts and equipment
  • Logistics support materials

Operational Advantages

  • Strategic Location

Proximity to oil fields in the Campos Basin.

  • Sheltered Bay

Operations in sheltered waters with greater safety.

  • Urban Connectivity

Easy access to urban and industrial centers.
