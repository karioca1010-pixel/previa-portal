Port of Angra dos Reis

Strategic port terminal with a distinctive geographic position for offshore operations in the Santos Basin, offering privileged proximity to the main pre-salt fields and significantly lower operating costs for the oil sector.

Request more information

Location and Access

The Port of Angra dos Reis is strategically located on the southern coast of Rio de Janeiro state, with a privileged geographic position to serve offshore operations in the Santos Basin. Its location is close to major capitals and the main pre-salt fields, directly reducing operating costs.

Close to the main pre-salt fields (Santos and Campos Basins), reducing operating costs.

Easy access via privatized highways (BR-101, free flow system).

Distances: 180 km from Rio de Janeiro, 360 km from São Paulo, 6.4 km from Angra airport.

Potential reactivation of the rail link to southern Minas Gerais, an important export and import hub.

Facilities and Equipment

Port Infrastructure

  • 400-meter Linear Quay

Suitable length for berthing offshore support vessels and medium-sized ships.

  • 8.53-meter draft

Operating depth certified by NPCP-RJ for berthing at zero tide.

  • 10-meter Access Channel

Depth of the maritime channel along its entire length for safe navigation.

Storage Areas

  • 65,000 m² of Storage Area

Ample space for storing offshore materials and equipment.

  • 2,500 m² of Covered Area

Protected storage with room for expansion as demand requires.

Ground Capacity

  • Pier: 30 tonnes/m²

Adequate strength for port operations and cargo handling.

  • Backup area: 60 tonnes/m²

High support capacity for heavy equipment and storage.

Customs Facilities

  • Bonded Area

Declaratory act that allows simulated imports and exports through in-person and virtual DAC (on request).

Strategic Advantages

  • Proximity to Pre-salt Fields

Privileged location reduces operating costs and travel time.

  • Easy Road Access

The privatized BR-101 ensures fast and efficient access.

  • Connectivity with Major Hubs

Proximity to Rio de Janeiro, São Paulo and Macaé supports integrated logistics.

Specialized Operations

  • Offshore support (handling of risers, mooring lines, torpedoes, pipelines, etc.).
  • Handling of general cargo and bulk (minerals, coke, salt, bran, coils, billets).
  • Materials management, integrated logistics and storage.
  • Water supply, fuel, waste management, inspection and preservation of equipment.
  • 24×7 operations with an experienced, certified team (ISPS Code).

Operational and QHSE advantages

  • Sole port operator on site.
  • Team trained and authorized to stop unsafe operations (Stop Authority).
  • Recognition for safety excellence (zero accidents and more than 671,000 hours worked in 2023).
  • Community integration and commitment to the environment and sustainability.
