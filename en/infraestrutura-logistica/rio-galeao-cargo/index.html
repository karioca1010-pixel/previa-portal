Rio Galeão Cargo

Rio Galeão Cargo has established itself as one of Latin America's main cargo terminals and a strategic pillar for those who import and export through the State of Rio de Janeiro. With a shareholder base made up of Vinci Compass and Changi Airports International, the concessionaire combines financial strength with the operational expertise of one of the world's largest airport operators. This arrangement, together with the know-how gained over more than 10 years of operation in Rio de Janeiro, supports an international standard infrastructure, high operational efficiency and a robust air network, positioning the terminal as an agile, safe gateway connected to the main global markets.

Location and Access

Rio Galeão Cargo is strategically located at Rio de Janeiro International Airport, Galeão, with international air access, road access and integration with the main logistics corridors. Its infrastructure allows 24 hour operations with direct connection between climate-controlled areas, yards and specialized docks.

Facilities and Equipment

Infrastructure designed to serve all segments

Privatized in 2014, the Rio Galeão cargo terminal has gone through a broad investment cycle that makes it one of the most complete in the country today. Planned investments reach R$ 5.2 billion through 2039, consolidating a modern, automated environment ready to serve operations of all sizes and complexities.

Certified excellence and international standards

Rio Galeão Cargo maintains a continuous focus on compliance and integrity. Its operations are certified and recognized by global bodies and organizations:

  • First airport in the Americas with IATA
  • CEIV Pharma
  • ISO 9001:2015
  • OEA (Authorized Economic Operator)
  • Certificate of Merit of the Organization
  • World Customs Cup
  • ANVISA Good Practices

The terminal also has dedicated pharmacists, a trained CEIV team, advanced temperature monitoring and a logistics efficiency program (PEL) that integrates the entire chain, reducing bottlenecks and optimizing lead times.

One of the largest air cargo hubs in the country

The air network at Rio Galeão is one of the factors that most boosts the state's competitiveness as a foreign trade corridor.

  • 24 international destinations
  • 27 domestic destinations
  • 22 airlines operating at the terminal

The airport's strategic position also allows it to act as a logistics hub with fast connections to the country's main industrial centers such as São Paulo, Minas Gerais, Espírito Santo, Santa Catarina and Goiás, and it is able to serve a wide variety of industries in the state of Rio de Janeiro, especially the pharmaceutical, automotive and aerospace industries present in the state.

Its structural highlights include:

  • 55 thousand m² of bonded storage area
  • 22,350 m³ of cold storage chambers More than 10,000 pallet positions, including 2,165 climate-controlled
  • Automated stacker crane system for pharmaceutical cargo
  • 9 positions dedicated to wide-body freighter aircraft
  • More than 40 docks, including temperature-controlled docks
  • Domestic warehouses operated by LATAM, GOL and Azul
  • 4,000-meter runway, the longest in Brazil, able to receive any cargo aircraft

This structure allows the terminal to operate efficiently in sectors such as oil and gas, pharmaceuticals, aerospace, automotive, textiles, e-commerce and high-value cargo, with dedicated logistics products for each need.
