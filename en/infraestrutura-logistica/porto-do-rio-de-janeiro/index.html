Port of Rio de Janeiro

Strategic port terminal with robust infrastructure and privileged rail connectivity, offering significant logistics advantages for moving cargo from the interior of the country, especially from the Paraíba Valley and southern Minas Gerais.

Request more information

Location and Access

The Port of Rio de Janeiro is strategically located on Guanabara Bay, with integrated sea, road and rail access. Its infrastructure provides direct connection to the country's main logistics corridors, offering significant competitive advantages over other ports.

Sea access through Guanabara Bay

Privileged rail connectivity

Integration with federal and state highways

Proximity to production centers in the interior

Rail Connectivity

  • Rail Competitive Advantage

Shorter distances to the main producing regions compared with other ports: the Port of Rio de Janeiro offers logistics advantages over the Port of Santos. From the Paraíba Valley (SP), the distance is 8 km shorter. From southern Minas Gerais (MG), the difference is 173 km less, making access to the Port of Rio significantly shorter.
