Rio Brasil Terminal

High-capacity container terminal with modern infrastructure and international certifications, specialized in handling diverse cargo including chemicals, reefers and special cargo, with integrated rail and road connectivity.

Request more information

Location and Access

Rio Brasil Terminal is located in the Port of Rio de Janeiro, with sea access via Guanabara Bay and privileged land connectivity. Its infrastructure allows integrated operations across sea, rail and road modes, with full customs facilities.

100% bonded area

Internal rail spur

Direct road access

Proximity to domestic and international airports

Facilities and Equipment

Handling Equipment

  • 05 STS (Ship to Shore Crane/Portêineres)

Container cranes with reach of up to 65 meters (23 rows) and capacity of up to 80 tons for operating large vessels.

  • 16 RTG/ERTG (12 electric)

Yard cranes of up to 45 tons, 12 of them electric units for sustainable operations.

  • 09 Reach Stackers

Equipment for agile container handling in the yard and storage areas.

Specialized Infrastructure

  • 270 Reefer Container Outlets

Complete system for monitoring and maintaining refrigerated cargo with temperature control.

  • Climate-Controlled Chamber

Temperature-controlled environment with capacity for 500 pallet positions for sensitive cargo.

  • Warehouse Exclusive to Chemical Cargo

Specialized facility with 1,200 pallet positions for chemical products with all the necessary certifications.

Certifications and Licenses

  • ISPS CODE Certified Terminal

International port security certification.

  • ISO 9001 and 14001 certifications

Quality and environmental management.

  • Specialized LicensesANVISA (pharmaceutical and sanitary products)
  • Army (controlled products)
  • Ministry of Agriculture (agricultural products)

Special Areas

  • Segregated Area for IMDG Cargo

Specialized zone for dangerous goods in line with the international code.

  • General Warehouse

Static capacity of 480 pallets for various cargo.

Customs Facilities

  • Sheltered Area for Customs Inspection
  • Dedicated Rooms for Receita Federal
  • Facilities for the Ministries of Health and Agriculture
  • Dedicated Spaces for Customs Brokers
