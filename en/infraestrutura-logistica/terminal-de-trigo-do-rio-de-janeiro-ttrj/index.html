Rio de Janeiro Wheat Terminal, TTRJ

Terminal specialized in handling and storing wheat, with high unloading capacity, silo storage and road loading, equipped with automated systems for efficient operations and quality control.

Request more information

Location and Access

The Rio de Janeiro Wheat Terminal is strategically located in the Port of Rio de Janeiro, with direct sea access via Guanabara Bay and road connectivity for distribution in the domestic market. Its infrastructure allows the receipt of bulk carriers and efficient distribution to the consumer market.

Road connectivity for distribution

Facilities and Equipment

Unloading System

  • 02 Ship unloaders

Equipment with a capacity of 300 tons per hour each, totaling 600 ton/h of unloading capacity.

  • Plank Target: 8,000 ton/h

Operational target for maximum efficiency in unloading bulk carriers.

Storage System

  • 03 Silo cells

Vertical storage system with an individual capacity of 19,100 tonnes each.

  • Total Capacity: 57,300 tons

Safe storage with quality control and preservation of product characteristics.

Quality Control

  • Monitoring Systems
  • Humidity and Temperature Control
  • Laboratory Analysis
  • Full Traceability

Loading System

  • 02 Road scales of 25 meters

Equipment for precise weighing of trucks during loading.

  • Loading Flow: 600 ton/h

Internal Transport System
