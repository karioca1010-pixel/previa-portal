Steel Products Terminal

Terminal specialized in handling and storing steel products, with robust infrastructure, direct multimodal access and strategic proximity to airports, offering integrated logistics solutions for the steel sector.

Request more information

Location and Access

The Steel Products Terminal is strategically located in the Port of Rio de Janeiro, with direct rail and road access at the terminal. Its privileged location is close to domestic and international airports, facilitating integrated operations and efficient distribution.

Facilities and Equipment

Port Infrastructure

  • 740-meter Quay

Suitable length for berthing ships specialized in steel products.

  • Draft of up to 9 meters

Operating depth to receive specialized medium-sized vessels.

Storage Areas

  • 50,000 m² of Bonded Area

Primary zone with full customs facilities for steel products.

  • 70,000 m² of Backup Area

Additional space for storage, processing and preparation of cargo.

Multimodal Connectivity

  • Direct Rail Access

Direct connection to the national rail network, allowing products to be received and dispatched by rail.

  • Direct Road Access

Easy access for trucks, enabling efficient door-to-door operations.

Logistics Advantages

  • Proximity to Airports

Strategic location close to Rio de Janeiro's domestic and international airports.

  • Modal Integration

Easy transfer between maritime, rail, road and air modes.

Operational Facilities

  • Specialized Equipment

Infrastructure suited to handling heavy steel products.

  • Processing Areas

Spaces for processing and preparing products according to customer specifications.

  • Quality Control

Facilities for inspection and certification of steel products.
