Porto Sudeste

High-capacity port terminal specialized in handling dry and liquid bulk, with modern infrastructure on Ilha da Madeira and a current capacity of 50 million tonnes per year, licensed to expand to 100 million tonnes.

Request more information

Location and Access

Porto Sudeste is located on Ilha da Madeira, on the southern coast of Rio de Janeiro state, with robust multimodal access infrastructure. The terminal has new facilities built specifically to meet the project's needs, ensuring efficient and safe cargo transport.

Strategic location on Ilha da Madeira

Robust road access infrastructure

Integrated rail connectivity

Direct sea access to the Atlantic Ocean

Operational Capacity

  • 50 Million Tons/Year

Current capacity for handling dry and liquid bulk.

Authorization to expand operational capacity.

Storage System

  • 32 m yard

Storage capacity of 1.3 million tons, located 32 meters above sea level.

Handling Equipment

  • 6 Storage Areas

Three areas in each yard for efficient organization of materials.

  • 4 Stacker-Reclaimer Machines

Two machines per yard with high operational capacity.

High efficiency for receiving and storing materials.

  • Reclaiming Capacity: 12,000 ton/hour

System optimized for loading and dispatching cargo.

Maritime Infrastructure
