MRS Logística

MRS Logística offers complete and efficient solutions for rail transport of containers and general cargo, connecting the main production and consumption hubs of the Southeast to the most important ports in the region. With a robust rail network and strategically located terminals, MRS is the ideal partner to optimize your business logistics.

Request more information

Location and Access

MRS Logística operates a 1,643 km rail network in one of the most strategic regions of Brazil, which concentrates about 50% of the national GDP. With robust infrastructure, MRS offers direct, integrated access to the main production and industrial hubs of the states of Minas Gerais, Rio de Janeiro and São Paulo, connecting them to the largest ports in the Southeast.

Direct access with no queues to the five largest port complexes in the region: Santos (SP), Itaguaí (RJ), Guaíba (RJ), Sudeste (RJ) and Rio de Janeiro (RJ), essential for export flows.

The fleet of approximately 18,000 wagons and more than 700 locomotives allows the transport of large volumes, including iron ore, steel products, cement, grains and containers.

The network connects major production and consumption centers, such as Greater São Paulo, the Paraíba Valley, the Rio de Janeiro metropolitan region and the iron quadrangle of Minas Gerais, ensuring an efficient logistics flow.

Our Services

  • Container Express Train Service with a daily schedule and fixed departure times, dedicated to transporting containers with varied cargo. Ideal for those seeking speed and regularity, including the cross-bank service at the Port of Santos.
  • General Cargo Train Flexible solution for transporting a variety of goods, such as steel products, cement and agricultural commodities. The trains have scheduled stops to serve multiple terminals along the route.

Safety First

  • "Door to Door" Containers: Positioning that prevents access to the cargo during transport.
  • Controlled Operation: Stops restricted to terminals and crew change points.
  • Armed Escort: Additional security on strategic stretches mapped in advance.
