Sepetiba Tecon Container Terminal

Strategic hub for port operations with high container volumes, ready to receive New Panamax class ships, with modern infrastructure and operating depth of up to 20 meters.

Request more information

Location and Access

Sepetiba Tecon is located on Sepetiba Bay, RJ, with highly structured rail, road and sea access. Its infrastructure allows agile, integrated service between the yard, warehouses and backup area, with direct connection to rail spurs.

Channel with a depth of 20 meters

Capacity for up to 60 railcars in the port area

Two rail spurs directly connecting the warehouses
