Porto do Açu

Strategic hub for port operations with high container volumes, ready to receive New Panamax class ships, with modern infrastructure and operating depth of up to 20 meters.

Request more information

Location and Access

Porto do Açu is strategically located on the northern coast of the State of Rio de Janeiro, with direct sea access to the Atlantic Ocean and land connectivity to the main production centers. The complex offers world-class infrastructure for large-scale industrial and port operations.
