Gávea Group

GÁVEA GROUP operates in the logistics and port infrastructure sector, offering complete solutions for the Oil & Gas industry and international trade. With an integrated operating model, we serve clients throughout Brazil, with a strategic presence in Rio de Janeiro, Espírito Santo and the Northeast.

MRM

MRM Miami offers reliable and efficient storage and distribution solutions, focused on optimizing your logistics operation.

We offer complete services in repackaging, storage, dangerous goods inspection and cargo preparation, as well as full coordination of distribution and all processes related to these operations. We operate in ports and airports across Brazil, with headquarters in Rio de Janeiro and branches strategically located in different regions of the country.

Our logistics coverage is global, supported by a network of more than 9,000 representatives in about 180 countries. In addition, MRM Miami has an active presence in the main international logistics associations, which allows us to transport goods to and from anywhere in the world, with safety, agility and reliability.

Air Sea

Founded in 2020, Air Sea Logistics is a company specialized in integrated international logistics, with strategic operations across air, sea and road modes, serving clients in Brazil and abroad. With a highly qualified team and a solid global network of partners, we develop customized, safe and efficient logistics solutions, aligned with the particularities of each operation and the demands of foreign trade.

Our mission is to offer agile and reliable logistics services, anticipating scenarios, optimizing routes and reducing costs, always focused on an excellent customer experience. Our vision is to establish ourselves as a reference in integrated logistics, recognized for operational quality, innovation and closeness to the customer. Guided by values such as reliability, efficiency, transparency and operational excellence, we constantly seek to improve processes and generate sustainable value for our partners.

Ziranlog

Ziranlog operates as an integrated logistics operator, offering transport, storage and bonded terminal solutions. With a strong presence in Rio de Janeiro, it contributes to the efficiency of the logistics chain and to the development of businesses linked to foreign trade and the offshore industry.
