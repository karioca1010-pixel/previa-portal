Air Sea Logistics

Integrated solutions across air, sea and road modes to speed up your foreign trade operations in Brazil and worldwide. Air Sea Logistics turns the complexity of international trade into simple, agile and efficient processes. Combining operational intelligence, a solid global network of partners and a highly qualified team, we develop tailor-made strategies for your cargo, whether by land, air or sea. Our commitment is to anticipate scenarios, optimize routes and reduce your operating costs, ensuring a transparent experience from shipment to delivery.
