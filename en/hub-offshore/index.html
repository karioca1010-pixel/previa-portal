Rio de Janeiro and the offshore industry

The offshore sector in Rio de Janeiro accounts for most of Brazil's oil and natural gas production (almost 80%), establishing the region as Brazil's main offshore exploration hub. The relevance of this activity stems from technological development in ultra-deepwater extraction, with strong economic and logistics impact on several fronts. As a result, the entire offshore logistics chain has great prominence and relevance in the state of Rio de Janeiro. Extraction Volume: The state leads the country's hydrocarbon supply by a wide margin, driven by pre-salt reserves located in the Campos and Santos basins. Infrastructure and Logistics: Municipalities in the Norte Fluminense and the Metropolitan Region serve as key operating bases, concentrating the overwhelming majority of the air and sea traffic supporting offshore platforms. Naval Chain and Governance: The state has port facilities and shipyards focused on the maintenance and construction of support vessels and floating units, and also has a public management structure geared to developing the maritime economy. Diversification Potential: The existing technical infrastructure and skilled workforce open room for new investment in offshore renewable energy, such as offshore wind generation.
