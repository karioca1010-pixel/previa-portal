TPAR

TPAR is a terminal specialized in Port Logistics and Onshore Logistics Base services. We are duly licensed to support the execution of Offshore Projects. The Port of Angra dos Reis is a Public Port, leased to the Splenda group through its subsidiary TPAR, Terminal Portuário de Angra dos Reis S/A. The main comparative advantages are its strategic location, close to the pre-salt, which allows fuel consumption to be cut by up to 40%, the 400 meter quay, and a draft able to receive large vessels such as PLSVs, drilling rigs, MODUs and Heavy Lift ships.

Our Services:

TPAR specializes in logistics support for offshore activities and in handling project cargo and general cargo. It has a team of highly qualified professionals and offers complete infrastructure to serve its clients.

Port Operation

The quay is 400 linear meters long, with bollards every 30 meters. All are certified with a capacity of 55 and 65 SWL.

Waste Treatment

TPAR has qualified partners for the transport and final disposal of waste of any class, as well as for tank cleaning.

Offshore Support Base

TPAR specializes in handling pipes, risers, project cargo and supporting offshore movements.

Fluids Plant

Our fluids plant began operating in 2011 with storage capacity of 43,000 barrels and production of 6,000 barrels per day.

Water, Power and Diesel Supply

Ability to meet our clients' required demand at any point in the Port.

Cargo Storage

TPAR has an area of 88,000 m², of which 48,000 m² is bonded yard, 21,000 m² is non-bonded yard, and there is a 2,250 m² masonry warehouse.
