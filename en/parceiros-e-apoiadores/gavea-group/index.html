Gavea Group

In the market since 2010, Gavea Log is an established project management operator for the Oil and Gas and international trade markets, offering in-house solutions across its different facilities and infrastructure. With centralized coordination of all areas and integration of the various services required by these segments, we provide complete solutions, integrating maritime, port and land logistics. Preserving the health and safety of our employees, as well as the environment, is the rigorous focus in carrying out our activities. We also follow strict standards of ethics and compliance in conducting our business.
