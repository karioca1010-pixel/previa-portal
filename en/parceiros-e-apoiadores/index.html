Angra dos Reis Port Terminal

The mission of Terminal Portuário de Angra dos Reis (TPAR) is to offer safe, efficient and sustainable logistics solutions for the oil and gas chain. With a strategic location close to the main pre-salt fields, TPAR reduces operating costs and stands out as the only port operator at the site, ensuring agility in offshore support and general cargo handling.

Its integrated operations cover 24x7 port operations, storage over an area of 88,839 m² and environmental management, actively contributing to the sustainable development of Rio de Janeiro. The commitment to excellence is reflected in the zero accidents milestone and in the promotion of innovation and social and environmental responsibility.

Brasil Export, National Forum on Logistics, Infrastructure and Transport

Consolidated as the most comprehensive movement in the logistics, infrastructure and transport sector in Brazil, Brasil Export is a permanent, multisector and unifying forum. It brings together the main authorities and business leaders to foster dialogue, encourage innovation and promote knowledge. All Brasil Export initiatives aim to help expand national infrastructure through diversified investment, within a sustainable development policy. With national reach and regional presence, Brasil Export is structured through regional and technical councils that bring together professionals and government agents to promote debate, propose public policies and stimulate investment in sustainable infrastructure. These councils represent the different regions of Brazil, North, Northeast, Center-West, Southeast, South and Mercosur, with strategic themes such as energy, legal and regulatory affairs, ESG and innovation, among others.

Brazil-Canada Chamber of Commerce, BCCC

The Brazil-Canada Chamber of Commerce, BCCC is a not-for-profit organization dedicated to fostering and strengthening the economic and commercial ties between Brazil and Canada. With a mission to promote bilateral trade, investment, and partnerships, the BCCC serves as a bridge between the two nations, helping businesses, organizations, and governments collaborate more effectively across borders. By providing key resources, strategic insights, and valuable connections, the Chamber supports its members in expanding their commercial activities and achieving long-term success. For over 50 years, the BCCC has played a pivotal role in connecting leaders from the private and public sectors in Brazil and Canada. Through networking, advocacy, and market intelligence, the Chamber helps members identify opportunities, overcome challenges, and enhance their business interests. With a strong focus on facilitating dialogue, the BCCC empowers companies to grow in dynamic, sustainable, and competitive markets. Its initiatives include industry-specific events, trade missions, policy advocacy, and thought leadership designed to drive impactful collaborations and foster innovation. As a nationwide organization, the BCCC operates across Canada, with active chapters in Ontario (Toronto, Ottawa, Kitchener-Waterloo, and Peterborough), British Columbia, New Brunswick, Nova Scotia, Manitoba, and Alberta (Calgary and Edmonton). It also maintains five dynamic committees that address critical sectors: Mining, Trade & Investment, Innovation Science & Technology (IST), ESG & Sustainability, and Education. These committees provide members with targeted opportunities for engagement and thought exchange in key areas shaping the future of bilateral business relations. Through its comprehensive programs and strategic focus, the BCCC remains committed to advancing sustainable growth and reinforcing the ties between Brazil and Canada.

ECOBRAX, Emission Reduction Solutions

Ecobrax is a Brazilian company founded in 2023, specialized in the development, manufacturing and supply of ARLA 32 and ARLA 40, solutions aimed at reducing nitrogen oxide (NOx) emissions in engines equipped with SCR technology.

With a proposal based on quality, efficiency, sustainability and logistics excellence, Ecobrax serves different segments of the economy, including carriers and fleets, agribusiness, industry, fuel networks and the offshore and maritime market. The company has a plant in Aracruz/ES, strategically positioned to efficiently serve different regions of Brazil.

Through SCR technology, ARLA acts directly on exhaust gas treatment, converting NOx into substances with lower environmental impact, such as nitrogen and water vapor. ARLA 32 is intended mainly for heavy vehicles and road fleets, while ARLA 40 serves applications such as marine vessels, agricultural machinery and industrial equipment.

Ecobrax seeks to contribute to a cleaner and more efficient operation, helping companies with emissions reduction, compliance with environmental standards and building more sustainable operations.

The Programa de Logística Verde Brasil (PLVB®) is the only Brazilian sustainability program for logistics and freight transport aimed at carriers, shippers and logistics operators who lead sustainability in logistics activities through an ESG approach (Corporate Governance of Environmental and Social initiatives), adoption of the SDGs (Sustainable Development Goals) and a smart approach to reaching net zero carbon emissions (Smart Approach to Net Zero Emissions) through good practices and engagement in the energy and technological transition.
