Privacy Policy, Portal Comex Rio

1. Our commitment to your privacy

Portal Comex Rio values your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data, in compliance with the General Data Protection Law (LGPD, Law No. 13,709/2018). This document describes how we collect, use, store and protect your information when you use our website.

2. Personal data collected

We may collect the following data:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone
  • Company and job title (when provided)
  • IP address, geolocation and browsing data (cookies)
  • Information entered in contact forms, comments or newsletter sign-ups

3. Purpose of collection

We use the data collected for the following purposes:

  • Contact you upon request
  • Send content, newsletters and informational materials
  • Improve the user experience and site performance
  • Analyze access metrics and statistics
  • Comply with legal and regulatory obligations

4. Consent

By browsing our website and providing your data through forms, you declare that you are aware of and agree to this Privacy Policy.

You may withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us through the channels provided on this page.

5. Data sharing

Your data may be shared with:

  • Technology and hosting service providers
  • Email marketing and analytics tools (e.g. Google Analytics, Mailchimp)
  • Public authorities, by legal or court requirement

We do not sell or trade personal data to third parties.

6. Cookies and tracking technologies

We use cookies to:

  • Improve your browsing
  • Save user preferences
  • Collect analytics data (Google Analytics)

You can manage or disable cookies directly in your browser. By continuing to browse our website, you agree to the use of cookies as described in this Policy.

7. Data subject rights

You have the right to:

  • Confirm whether your data is being processed
  • Access, correct or update your data
  • Request deletion or anonymization
  • Withdraw consent
  • Request data portability

To exercise your rights, send a request by email to: contato@portalcomexrio.com.br

8. Security and storage

We adopt technical and administrative measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, destruction, loss, alteration or leaks, including:

  • SSL Certificate
  • Backup and access control
  • Traffic monitoring

Your data is stored only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described here, or as required by law.

9. Data Protection Officer (DPO)

If you have questions about this Policy or your personal data, please contact our Data Protection Officer (DPO):

Name: LGPD Officer, Portal Comex Rio Email: contato@portalcomexrio.com.br

10. Changes to this Policy

This Policy may be updated at any time to reflect improvements or changes in our privacy practices. We recommend that you review it regularly.

11. Jurisdiction

Any disputes related to this Policy will be settled in the courts of the judicial district of Rio de Janeiro, RJ, waiving any other, however privileged.

This policy complies with LGPD (Law 13,709/2018) and reflects Portal Comex Rio's commitment to the transparency, security and privacy of your data.
