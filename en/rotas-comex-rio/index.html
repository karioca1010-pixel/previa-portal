Comex Rio Routes

Rio de Janeiro's logistics routes connect Brazil to the world efficiently. The ports of Rio de Janeiro state have privileged access to the main global markets.

Main Routes

Portal ComexRio Services

Portal ComexRio highlights the main logistics routes connecting Rio de Janeiro to the world, with competitive lead times and costs to meet import and export demand across the country.

Whether moving inputs, finished goods or consumer products, Rio de Janeiro is a strategic point to reach the main domestic and international markets, including connections to Buenos Aires, Uruguay and other Mercosur routes.
